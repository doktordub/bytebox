# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Contributors are expected to be respectful, direct, and constructive. Harassment, abuse,
discrimination, intimidation, and deliberately disruptive behavior are not acceptable.

## Expected behavior

- Assume good faith, but be precise.
- Keep technical disagreement factual.
- Protect private information, credentials, and security-sensitive details.
- Prefer actionable review feedback over personal criticism.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns to the repository owner or project maintainer. Reports should include
the date, location, involved parties, and any supporting context.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may restrict participation when
behavior violates this policy.
